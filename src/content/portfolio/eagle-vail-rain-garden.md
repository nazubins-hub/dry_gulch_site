## The problem

Runoff from the driveway and an upper-lot slope was pooling against the foundation every spring, and the client had already dealt with one basement moisture issue. A standard French drain would have moved the water but done nothing to slow or use it.

## The approach

The design regrades a shallow swale to intercept runoff before it reaches the house and routes it into a bioinfiltration basin sized for both peak storm volume and sustained spring snowmelt flow. The basin floor uses an engineered soil mix over the existing decomposed granite subsoil to get real infiltration instead of surface pooling.

## Planting

Basin floor: golden currant, red-twig dogwood, native sedges — species that tolerate brief inundation.
Basin slopes and surrounding beds: penstemon, blue grama, and rabbitbrush on drip irrigation, transitioning to unirrigated native grass at the property line.

## Result

Two full snowmelt seasons in, the basin has handled peak flow without overflow, and the foundation moisture issue has not recurred.
